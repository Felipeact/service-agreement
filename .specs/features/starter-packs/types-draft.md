# Starter packs: draft type lists

Draft by Claude, **not reviewed by a practitioner**. This is the list the plan's criteria count against (plan AC 1). Tasks and spellings are drafted during the build and go to the practitioner as a review sheet; nothing here is a code, interval or standard to rely on until they sign it off. Default months are a starting point for the estimator, and a visit can always be moved on the month grid.

Months are 1 to 12 (3,6,9,12 is quarterly in Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec; 4,10 is spring and fall; 10 is one annual visit in October, the same default the HVAC pack uses for annual items).

Marked **R** are the types the reviewer reads first: a wrong checklist there is a safety or health problem, not an inconvenience.

## Not in either pack, on purpose

Fire alarm, sprinkler and elevator equipment (fire and elevators wait for their own reviewer, roadmap decision 5), gas piping (a separate trade licence), medical gas, and anything weekly or every-N-weeks (a 12-month grid cannot say it; roadmap Phase 6).

## Where the draft comes from

Types follow the items commercial plumbing and electrical maintenance guides and public tenders list; intervals in those sources disagree and several are vendor marketing. The better sources the search found: Phoenix's interceptor maintenance brochure (30 days for a grease trap, 90 days for an interceptor, 6 to 12 months for lint or sand and oil), a county RFQ and a VA tender for annual transfer-switch and generator service, and an NFPA 70B reference for annual infrared scans. None is a Canadian requirement. Frequencies stay drafts until the reviewer confirms them for the province.
