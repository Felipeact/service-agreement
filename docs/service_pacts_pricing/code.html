ANSWERS TO QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about plans, limits, and how to get started. Still stuck? Talk to us.

What exactly counts towards an active agreement?expand_more

An active agreement is any signed or actively monitored service agreement in your registry. Drafts, expired agreements, and archived client files do not count toward your tier cap.

What happens when I exceed my monthly AI schedule imports?expand_more

You can top up instant import bundles right from the billing dashboard for $1.50 CAD per roster import, or seamlessly upgrade to the next tier without pausing your current workflows.

Does Service Pacts replace my dispatch or accounting software?expand_more

Service Pacts pairs directly alongside systems like ServiceTitan, Jonas, and QuickBooks. We handle the agreement creation, automated renewals, equipment matrices, and customer video approvals that trade CRMs miss.

Where is data hosted and is Service Pacts Canadian compliant?expand_more

100% of customer records and mechanical registries are hosted inside tier-3 Canadian data centers (Calgary & Toronto) compliant with SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, and PIPEDA privacy requirements.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing anytime?expand_more

Yes. You can switch to annual billing at any point to lock in 20% savings, or cancel with zero long-term commitment penalties.
